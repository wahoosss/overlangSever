Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026 | Applies to: OverLang

The App translates on-screen text in real time and paints it back in place; recognition and translation both happen on your device. It runs on a "free + ads" model, so it connects to the internet to load ads, and, with your consent, reports necessary usage statistics and crash information. The App is distributed via Google Play, and its privacy compliance follows the Google Play policies and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Below is a quick overview first, then the details.

0. 30-second overview

  • The screen content you share is read, recognized, translated, and painted on your device; it never leaves your device, processed frames are discarded immediately, and it is never used as a basis for ad targeting.
  • Translation history and screenshots are stored in the App's private directory on your device; no other app or website can read them. You can delete them one by one or all at once, and uninstalling removes them.
  • The App stays free by serving ads through Google AdMob; ads use information such as device, network, and advertising identifiers (GAID).
  • On first launch you can choose to accept or decline: Accept = personalized ads + analytics enabled; Decline = non-personalized ads only, analytics disabled. Either way, all on-device translation features keep working.
  • We do not collect your contacts, SMS, call logs, photos, or precise location, and we do not read passwords or payment details you type in other apps.
  • Apart from the information needed for ads and analytics, the only other network activity is downloading offline models: translation language packs (Google) and OCR script models (developer mirror). The only thing sent is a request for "the model file for a given language".

1. How we obtain your consent

On first launch, the App shows this agreement on the last screen of the guide, with a confirmation box that is checked by default. Your consent is based on your active tap of "Get started", not on the default state:

  • Keep it checked and tap "Get started" = Accept: enables personalized ads and analytics (Google Firebase Analytics);
  • Uncheck it, then tap "Get started" = Decline: analytics are disabled and your usage and crash data are not collected; to keep the App free, ads are still shown, but only non-personalized ones;
  • Accepting or declining does not affect your use of the translation features; all on-device capabilities remain available.

About "non-personalized ads": when an ad is requested and shown, the ad SDK still transmits device, network, and advertising-identifier information to Google for delivery, frequency capping, attribution, and anti-fraud; this information is not used to build an interest profile or for personalized targeting. "Non-personalized" limits how ads are targeted, not whether data is sent at all.

European Economic Area (EEA) and UK users: you will additionally see a standard consent interface provided by Google (User Messaging Platform, UMP). Your choice there takes precedence over the checkbox above and directly determines whether ads are personalized and whether analytics are enabled. You can withdraw or change that choice at any time: an entry to reopen the UMP privacy-options interface is provided at the top-right of the App's "Privacy Policy & Terms of Use" page.

2. What information we collect and what it is used for

2.1 Screen content: stays on-device, discarded after use

After you authorize screen recording: frames are read one by one → text is recognized on your device → translated on your device → the translation is painted back in place on the overlay → that frame is then discarded. Recognition and translation all happen on your device; no image, text, or translation is uploaded.

This path does not depend on any remote service. In airplane mode (with models already downloaded and no ads being shown), recognition and translation still work.

2.2 Translation history and screenshots: stored in your app-private directory

  • Each time a translation completes, the App records the original text, translation, position, and time in the device's app-private directory for review on the "Translation history" page; no other app or website can read these files;
  • If you enable "Save original image" in the settings on the "Translation history" page, that translation also saves a screenshot of the frame, likewise only in the private directory and not written to the system photo gallery;
  • The maximum number of history entries, whether to save original images, and clearing all history are all adjusted in the settings at the top-right of the "Translation history" page; lowering the maximum immediately trims the oldest entries and deletes their screenshots;
  • This content never enters the ads or analytics reporting path (see 2.3 and 2.4), and it is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear app data.

Reminder: screen translation reads everything shown on your screen, which may include messages others sent you. History entries and screenshots are stored on your device like this, so please keep your device safe and check this content before sharing or backing up your phone.

2.3 Information used for ad delivery (happens whether or not you accept personalization)

To load and serve ads and to perform de-duplication, attribution, and anti-fraud, the ad SDK (Google AdMob) processes the following information:

  • Device and app information: device model, brand, OS version, screen resolution and pixel density, app version, language, and region;
  • Network information: network type (Wi-Fi / cellular), and the approximate region of the IP address (the full IP is not stored);
  • Advertising identifier: the device advertising ID (GAID) used for ad delivery and attribution, which you can reset or disable in system settings.

When you accept, Google may use the above information for personalized targeting; when you decline, ads are served non-personalized (see Section 1). Ad content does not read your screen, recognition results, translations, or history.

2.4 Information used for analytics and crash diagnosis (collected only after you accept)

Only after you make an accepting choice per Section 1 (for EEA/UK users, consenting to analytics in UMP) does the App enable Google Firebase Analytics and collect:

  • Usage information: feature tap counts, whether a translation succeeded or failed (result codes), and the duration ranges of the recognition and translation stages, as events;
  • Diagnostic information: crash stacks and where they occurred, for stability troubleshooting.

The reported usage information contains only behavioral values such as "which button was tapped" and "whether this translation succeeded"; it does not contain the screen content you shared, the recognized original text, translations, history fields, screenshots, or text position coordinates. When you decline, Firebase is not initialized, and not a single byte of the above usage or crash data leaves your device.

2.5 Download requests used to obtain language models

  • Translation language packs: Google ML Kit's model-download capability (via Google Play services) downloads the dictionary for your chosen target language, about 30 MB per language. The request includes device model, OS version, app package name, and the requested language and model version, and does not include your screen content, recognition results, translations, or identity information; processing and retention are described in the Google Privacy Policy;
  • OCR script models: recognition models for some languages are provided by a developer-hosted mirror, fetched over HTTPS. The request carries only "the model for a given script" and none of your image or text data.

2.6 What we do not collect

  • Identity information: name, phone number, email, address — we neither request nor collect these (the App has no account system and requires no sign-up or login);
  • Sensitive data: contacts, SMS, call logs, photos, and precise or approximate location — the App does not request these permissions and does not read them; passwords and payment details you type in other apps are not recognized, recorded, or uploaded by the App;
  • Device identifiers: the App itself does not request IMEI, Android ID, or other identifiers used for profiling; the advertising identifier (GAID) is used only by the ad SDK for delivery and attribution, and you can reset or disable it in system settings;
  • When you have not consented: no usage statistics or crash logs leave your device.

3. Permissions we request and why, and what they will not do

  • Screen recording (MediaProjection): reads the screen content you actively choose to share, to recognize text. Each start requires you to confirm in a system dialog; during the sharing session the system shows a persistent "sharing your screen" indicator, from which you can stop at any time;
  • Display over other apps (overlay): paints the translation in place over the original text and hosts the start / stop control buttons;
  • Foreground service: keeps translation running when you switch to other apps. This capability requires a persistent notification as its credential, but the App does not request the notification-display permission: on Android 13 and above, if you have not allowed the App to show notifications, this notification will not appear, and translation is unaffected — you can then tell it is running, and stop it, from the system's "sharing your screen" indicator and the on-screen floating control button;
  • Network access and network state: used to load ads, to report usage statistics after you consent, and to download the language packs and recognition models described in 2.5, and to tell you "whether there is no network or the connection is restricted" when a download fails;
  • The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, storage, or photo-gallery write permissions.

Recommendation: do not keep screen translation running for a long time on pages involving payments, ID documents, or others' privacy; if you leave history entries and screenshots on such pages, they are stored on your device. Please use this feature only on screens you genuinely need translated.

5. Storage, sharing, and protection of information

  • Storage location and duration: on-device data (preferences, translation history, screenshots, downloaded models) is stored in the app-private directory, where history is kept up to the maximum number of entries you set and the oldest are evicted beyond that; the ads and analytics data reported over the network is processed by Google under its policies, and the App's server-side reported data is retained for a maximum of 12 months, after which it is deleted or anonymized;
  • Transfer security: all network requests use HTTPS; ad and analytics requests do not carry your screen content, original text, translations, or history;
  • Sharing scope: shared with the third parties above only to the extent necessary to implement ads, analytics, and troubleshooting, and your personal information is never sold; where required by law or compelled by a competent authority, we may disclose it as required by law and notify you to the extent permitted;
  • Cross-border transfers: Google AdMob / Firebase / UMP may involve transferring data outside your country (e.g., to Google's global servers); we rely on this agreement and the applicable third party's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) and other compliance mechanisms as the basis for transfer;
  • Device backup: Android's "app data backup / transfer to a new device" is handled by your system settings and Google One, and may include the App's history data in your personal backup. This is not data collection by the App; you can turn off backup in system settings, or turn off "back up app data shared with me".
